Toolbar icon redesigned as a teal 2D bot face. The previous chrome-minion silhouette used an 88×120 portrait viewBox rendered at 18×24, which floated above the baseline shared by the surrounding toolbar icons (home, calendar, alarm, …) and read as a filled blob next to their stroked line-art. The new icon is a square 24×24 viewBox at 20×20 render size: rounded teal head + antenna in `#14B8A6` (Tailwind teal-500), white eyes and smile inside the silhouette so the face stays legible on both light and dark Logseq themes. The marketplace card icon (`./icon.png`) is unchanged.
